<h2 class="intro-greeting">Hi!</h2>

I am a Machine Learning Researcher, currently collaborating with [Avijit Maji](https://www.civil.iitb.ac.in/~avimaji/) at [IIT Bombay](https://www.iitb.ac.in/). I entered IIT Bombay intending to study civil engineering, but research changed the direction of my interests early on. From my second year, I worked on computational problems in transportation, including modeling traffic, vehicle interactions, and driver behavior, and became increasingly curious about the machine learning methods behind those applications. My undergraduate research work with Avijit Maji culminated in a [thesis](/projects/#vehicle-behavior) on ML for vehicle speed and trajectory prediction.

I later joined [Georgia Tech](https://www.gatech.edu/) for an [M.S. in Computational Science and Engineering](https://www.cse.gatech.edu/ms-computational-science-and-engineering) to gain a broader foundation in ML. I used the program to learn and work across several areas, including non-Euclidean representation learning, graph neural networks, federated learning, and computational simulation.

I then spent several years applying computational methods in transportation planning through travel-demand forecasting and model development at [Fehr & Peers](https://www.fehrandpeers.com/) and [TJKM](https://www.tjkm.com/services/travel-demand-modeling/). I did not leave transportation behind so much as follow the broader questions it introduced me to. Those questions now center on structure: how it appears in data, how models encode it, and how it can help us understand failures and make better decisions.

<p class="closing-line">I am most excited about learning systems that do more than predict: systems that can help us uncover patterns and better understand what gives rise to them.</p>

## Research

My research interests lie in reliable and trustworthy machine learning, meta-learning and AutoML, and graph and geometric representation learning.

### Current Projects

- **Tabular foundation model reliability:** Studying how high-confidence errors develop across model layers and whether internal representations contain useful information about unreliable predictions.

- **Data complexity and hyperparameter optimization:** Studying how properties of a dataset can inform decisions made before hyperparameter tuning.

### Earlier Work

In the past, I have explored non-Euclidean sentence representations, graph neural networks for imbalanced classification, personalized federated learning, agent-based simulation, long-horizon traffic-flow prediction, visual positioning, and vehicle-behavior modeling.

For more details, please see the [Projects page](/projects/).
